Strategic Recommendations

Actionable insights derived from the survey analysis.

01. Address Conversion Gap
West Bengal & Gujarat show high brand awareness but lower purchase intent. This is a conversion issue, not an awareness issue. Conduct targeted follow-ups to identify friction points.
02. Prioritize the 26–35 Cohort
This demographic represents the highest purchase intent but reports mid-level satisfaction. Identifying and fixing the experience gap here will drive the next wave of growth.
03. Upgrade Electronics Experience
Electronics maintains high average satisfaction but shows mixed sentiment at the lower end. Focus on post-purchase support and after-sales service to lift the floor.
04. Reinforce FMCG Loyalty
FMCG shows strong intent but weaker satisfaction. Bundle offers and loyalty incentives can convert intent into repeat purchases and stabilize confidence.
